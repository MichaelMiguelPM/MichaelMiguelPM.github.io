About

A product manager built for the AI era.

For five years at Brightidea, I owned enterprise B2B SaaS end to end, serving customers including Adobe, Amazon, Nike, and LinkedIn. I lead the full arc of the work: discovery with real customers, PRDs and prioritization, shipping, and the growth that follows.

What sets my work apart is an AI-native workflow. I compress the mechanical parts of product management, PRDs into tickets, research synthesis, prototyping, so the team spends its time on judgment and craft. I have taken a full product redesign from roughly 150 story points down to 35 by rethinking how the work gets done.

Outside of software, I served as a caregiver through Fight2Breathe, an experience that grounds how I think about products people depend on, and about building for the humans on the other side of the screen.

"Use AI for speed and volume. Keep human ownership of what is worth building."
A model once synthesized user feedback with total confidence and was wrong about why enterprise admins were stalling during setup. I only caught it on direct customer calls. AI multiplies output; the judgment stays mine.
