The Challenge

An enterprise product with no self-serve door

Brightidea sold through a sales-led enterprise motion. Meanwhile, tens of thousands of high-intent visitors reached the product and pricing pages every period, with no way to try the product themselves. I owned the self-serve Free Trial program: build a frictionless path from anonymous visitor to paying customer, running alongside the sales motion without cannibalizing enterprise deals.

No self-serve path

Prospects had to talk to sales before they could ever try the product, adding friction and losing top-of-funnel interest.

High-intent traffic wasted

Tens of thousands of visitors reached the site each period with no way to experience the product for themselves.

Sales-gated onboarding

Getting into a trial required back-and-forth with a rep, so signups stalled and only a fraction activated.

No instrumented funnel

Without visit → signup → activation → paid tracking, we could not see where prospects dropped off.

No path to purchase

Even engaged trial users had no frictionless, self-serve way to convert to a paid plan.

The Solution

A frictionless, self-serve path to paid

1 Self-serve signup

Public signup, no sales gate — a trial starts in minutes.

2 Guided activation

Onboarding that drives new users to first value fast.

3 Instrumented funnel

Heap tracking across visit → signup → activation → paid.

4 In-product upgrade

Self-serve checkout via FastSpring, no rep required.

5 Lifecycle nurture

HubSpot emails re-engage trial users to lift conversion.

6 Conversion analytics

Live funnel & revenue view to iterate on the rate.

Sequencing

Deliberate slicing: Now · Next · Later

Shipped the highest-leverage slices first — self-serve signup and instrumentation — so we could measure and convert immediately.

Next

Slice 2

Discovery

  • Self-serve expansion (seats/plans)
