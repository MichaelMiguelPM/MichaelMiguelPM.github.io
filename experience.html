Where I've made an impact.

Roles across Brightidea, Famous (now Amaze), and Fight2Breathe, with a few words from the people I worked with.

B

Brightidea

Enterprise innovation & ideation platform · San Francisco
Aug 2021 – Present

Senior Product Manager

I own Brightidea's Whiteboard end to end and shipped its full AI feature suite, AI brainstorming, one-click organize, smart matrix, and PRD and business-case generation, reaching 1,000+ unique users in the past year and becoming a lead topic in client conversations and sales enablement. I built an AI-augmented PM workflow with Claude and MCP that runs the full cycle, from call transcripts to PRDs to Jira tickets to clickable prototypes and local-dev hand-off. I delivered the Project Room redesign in 35 story points, work that traditionally runs 150+, by designing and prototyping it myself and cutting PRD-to-tickets from days to hours. I also own the Hackathon participant experience for events running 500+ participants, from the onboarding wizard to team building to Project Room.

"Miguel is an effective and creative, AI-fluent product leader … an asset to fast-moving, high-growth teams."

Matt Greeley · Founder & CEO, Brightidea

Product Manager

I owned end-to-end strategy, roadmap, and delivery for Whiteboard, Brightidea's real-time multiplayer ideation canvas used by teams at Adobe, Amazon, Nike, and LinkedIn, joining to stand up the new product line. I built the self-serve trial-to-paid engine from scratch to $123K in net revenue on 43K form visits and 8,000 signups at 18% conversion, largely organic with minimal marketing. I own and continuously expand Brightidea's Free Trial program across 19 enterprise products, spanning sign-up forms, nurture emails, payment and product types, and template configuration. I implemented chatbots, onboarding flows, and in-app messaging to drive trial activation and conversion.

"What sets Miguel apart is his relentless focus on the end user. He's a constant advocate, making sure the user's perspective is in the room for every decision."

Katlin Arps · Brightidea
F

Famous (now Amaze)

Browser-based interactive design tool · San Francisco
Jan 2018 – Jan 2021

Product Manager

I owned product roadmaps and full-lifecycle execution for Famous, a browser-based tool for building interactive, animated designs, partnering with design and engineering on nearly every major product decision. I authored PRDs and prioritized the feature backlog, translating user needs and product vision into clear, buildable specs. I shipped Asset Bar, a quick-access tool surface that let new users create designs dramatically faster. I led the Animation and Interaction Refresh, streamlining the core creative workflow so users could build interactive designs in minutes.

"Every company has that one person that is the heart of the culture. Miguel is that person for me."

Steve Newcomb · Founder & CEO, Famous

Content Producer

I produced Famous's educational content library, FAQs, video guides, and full-length design-build tutorials, that helped new users get productive faster. I translated a complex creative tool into clear, approachable learning material that supported onboarding and activation. My tutorials doubled as enablement and demand content, showing prospects and users exactly what the product could do.

"He is more than just an All-Star content producer; he is a key member of the product team and central to the Famous company culture."

Sara Osborn · Famous (now Amaze)

Community Manager

I hands-on onboarded every early user from the first alpha through beta user #200, turning their feedback into signal that shaped early product decisions. I ran a weekly Friday all-hands, bringing in designers from major tech companies to share their careers and work and to build community around the product. I served as the connective tissue between our earliest users and the team, keeping the community engaged as the product matured.

Fight2Breathe

Caregiving & 501(c)(3) advocacy · San Francisco
Jan 2014 – Jan 2018

Caregiver & Advocate

I was the primary caregiver to a Cystic Fibrosis patient, helping take her from surviving to thriving through hospice, two double-lung transplants, and 70+ procedures. I served as Creative Director for the organization's 501(c)(3) charity arm, producing branded apparel and media content. I led partnerships for events, public speaking engagements, community gatherings, and giving-back initiatives. This is where I learned to make high-stakes decisions with empathy and clarity under pressure, the foundation for how I build today.
