AI Rebooking (mobile)

A clickable mobile prototype of an AI-assisted rebooking flow for corporate travelers, built with Claude Code. When a flight is cancelled, the AI has already checked the traveler, the company policy and the deadline, and held the best seat. The traveler confirms in one tap. Agents only see the exceptions.

About this prototype

What you're looking at

Theo, a Senior Scientist at Halvorsen Biotech, has his Sunday evening flight from Los Angeles to Denver cancelled a week out, and he has to be in Denver by midnight for a client workshop. Instead of a cancellation notice and a phone queue, he gets the top three United nonstops that fit his profile, his company's policy and his deadline, with the best one already held. He is one of 49 travelers with three or more workable United options.

One-tap rebooking

The best in-policy flight on top, seat already held, with a plain note on the trade-off.

Why these options?

Shows the reasoning: the policy line it applied, the deadline it protected, and what it left out.

Policy-aware choices

Options over the $200 limit are routed to the travel manager for approval automatically.

Ask for something else

Earlier, later, nonstop, another airport or another day, then search again.

Graceful handoff

If nothing fits, a named agent takes over with full context. The seat stays held.

Exceptions-only agent view

Agents see only the cases that need them, plus what the AI handled, with Undo.

Tap through it as the traveler. Confirm the recommended flight, pick the Business seat to see it go to the travel manager, or choose Find other options and pick Different day to see the handoff to a person. The travel agency's side, where only the exceptions land, opens separately from See the agent's view.
