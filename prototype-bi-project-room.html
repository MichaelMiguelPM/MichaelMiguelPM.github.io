About this prototype

What you're looking at

A clickable desktop rebuild of the Brightidea Project Room, the enterprise collaboration space I redesigned in the Project Room v2 initiative. It turns a bare room, just a name, one member, and an empty description, into a space that is useful, social, and self explanatory the moment it exists.

Project Information

Editable hero image, project name, member count, and description.

Quick Actions bar

Quick creation of key objects: Whiteboard, Memo, View Tasks, Files.

Most Recent items

Memos and whiteboards at a glance, timestamped, with direct Open links.

Activity + History feed

Real time log of activity, uploads, task completions, and whiteboard updates.

Team sidebar + chat

Avatars, names and roles always visible, an Add button, and real time team chat.

Initiative Templates

App based reusable components that give users valuable data and info.
