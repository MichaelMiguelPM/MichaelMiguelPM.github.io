Get to know a bit more about Miguel

Product Career

At Brightidea, I owned collaborative innovation products end to end, launching Hackathon programs, bringing AI into the Whiteboard, and shipping Project Room so teams could run ideation at scale, for customers including Adobe, Amazon, Nike, and LinkedIn. At Famous, I led customer onboarding and helped develop a high-end design tool, learning what it takes to make a premium creative product feel effortless.

Early Career

Before product, I was a caregiver through Fight2Breathe, absorbing heavy medical knowledge and making decisions in life-or-death situations, the root of my bias toward empathy and clarity under pressure. With Canvas Surfboard, I learned to create hard physical products, taught myself CAD design, and figured out how to sell and run a business from the ground up.

Personal

Volleyball is where I sharpen team building, dedication, and execution, including coaching at the high-school level. Surfing is the counterweight, a solo practice that demands self-awareness and determination, right down to shaping my own boards.
