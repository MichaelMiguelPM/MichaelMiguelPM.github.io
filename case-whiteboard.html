Key Feature - Smart Matrix

A grid that never stopped evolving

Smart Matrix shipped in my first month at Brightidea and never stopped changing, from a static 2×2 grid to an AI-assisted, multiplayer scoring engine.

V1 launch, 2021

Prioritizing ideas on a board was manual and subjective. Nothing scored by placement, so rankings were hard to align on or defend.

Custom axes, 2023

The grid was locked to a fixed Impact/Effort frame, so teams whose criteria didn't fit those two axes couldn't use it for their own prioritization.

AI-assisted placement, 2025

Plotting linked ideas onto the matrix was a tedious manual sort, a real bottleneck once a board held dozens of linked Pipeline records.

Collaborative Smart Matrix, 2026

Scoring wasn't truly multiplayer. A team couldn't work a matrix together in real time, so sessions fell back to one person driving.

Key Feature - AI in Whiteboard

The largest bet: AI, built into the canvas

Generate ideas

Prompt-based idea generation, dropped straight onto the board as stickies.

Cluster & summarize

Auto-group stickies by author, keyword or theme, then summarize the cluster.

Smart Matrix auto-placement

AI plots linked ideas by Impact vs. Effort, no manual sorting.

Generate & edit content

Generate images, fix grammar, or rewrite tone, in place.

PRD generation

Turn a sticky into a full Product Requirements Document.

Build an App with AI

Hands the generated PRD to ChatGPT / Replit, pre-filled, for instant prototyping.
